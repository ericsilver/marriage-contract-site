Write to me

Anything sent here reaches me. You don’t have to say who you are. If you’d like a reply, leave a way to reach you.

Corrections are the most useful thing you can send (see how to report one). Please don’t send the details of your own case. I’m not a lawyer, I can’t advise you, and this form isn’t confidential the way a lawyer’s office is.

Messages travel through a form relay to my inbox. The relay sees the text you type and your connection details. I see only the text.
