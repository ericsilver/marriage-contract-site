The law in the book changes, and even a careful book has mistakes. Both kinds of correction are logged here, newest first. The year in the book's title tells you which edition you're holding.

How to report one

Write to me. You don't have to say who you are. The most useful report gives the chapter or page, a few words from the sentence, what it should say, and where you checked: a statute section, a case citation, or a link. Reports from lawyers about their own state are especially welcome, since no lawyer reviewed the book.
