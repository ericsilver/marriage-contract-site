If you read books like this one and might write about it, on Amazon or anywhere else, tell me a little about yourself. I can’t promise a copy to everyone who asks.

The terms are short

  1. A review isn’t required to get a copy, and nothing about what you write is up to me. If you hate it, say so.
  2. If you do review it, say that your copy was free.
  3. If you’re family or a close friend, read it anyway, but keep your review off Amazon, which doesn’t want reviews from people close to the author.

Messages travel through a form relay to my inbox. The relay sees the text you type and your connection details. I see only the text.
